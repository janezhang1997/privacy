Privacy Policy 隐私政策

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

Welcome to use JianTime (the “App”), a calendar habit tracker & note-taking tool. We respect and protect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our App.

Information We Access and How We Use It

Photo Library Permission

We request photo library access only when you actively select photos to attach to your check-in records. We only read the photos you manually choose. Selected photos are saved locally inside the App sandbox on your device. We will not scan your whole album, read other photos without your selection, or upload your images to any remote server.

You may revoke photo access permission anytime in iOS System Settings. After permission is revoked, you cannot add new photos, but your existing records remain intact.

User-generated Content (Local Storage)

All check-in entries, custom tags, text notes and preference settings you create are stored locally on your iPhone and will not be automatically uploaded to our servers. This data is only used for in-app display, calendar view and weekly/monthly statistics. Only you can view these data on this device.

You may delete single records or all data inside the App. Once deleted, records and attached images will be removed locally and cannot be recovered.

Permissions we do not request: location, microphone, contacts, health, or any other sensitive permission.

Data We DO NOT Collect

We do NOT collect your name, phone number, email, ID, location, contact list, device identifiers or any personal identifiable information. We do not integrate third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. We will never share, sell or transfer your data to any third parties.

Data Storage & Security

All your data stays locally on your device, protected by iOS sandbox isolation. We have no remote access to your App data.

If you enable iCloud Backup on your iPhone, iOS may include App data in your iCloud backup. This backup is controlled by Apple and governed by Apple’s privacy terms, outside of our control.

Children’s Privacy

This App is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Revoke photo library permission in iOS Settings;
  • Delete check-in records and attached images within the App;
  • Uninstall the App, which will erase all local App data;
  • Contact us via the email below for privacy inquiries.

Changes to This Policy

If we add new features in later versions, we will update this Privacy Policy and notify you inside the App.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at 1252964419@qq.com.

一、我们会获取的信息以及用途

相册（照片库）权限

仅在您主动选择上传图片添加到打卡记录时，才会向您申请相册访问权限。我们仅读取您选中的照片，照片仅保存在 App 在本机的沙盒目录内；不会主动扫描、读取您相册里其他照片，不会将图片上传、发送到外部服务器。

您可以随时在 iOS 系统设置中关闭相册权限。权限关闭后，您将无法继续添加图片，但原有记录不受影响。

应用内用户生成内容（本地存储）

您创建的打卡记录、自定义标签、备注文字、统计配置等内容，全部存储在您的 iPhone 本地，不会自动上传至我们的服务器。这些数据仅用于在 App 内展示、日历浏览、周/月数据分析，仅您本人可以在本机查看。

您可以在 App 内手动删除单条记录或者清空全部数据；删除后，对应记录和关联图片会从设备本地移除，无法恢复。

系统权限说明：本应用不会请求位置、麦克风、通讯录、健康等其他敏感权限。

二、我们不收集的信息

我们不会收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证、地理位置、通讯录、设备识别码等个人身份信息；不接入第三方统计、追踪、广告 SDK，不会跟踪您的使用行为，不会向任何第三方共享、出售您的任何数据。

三、数据存储与安全

所有数据存储在您的设备本地，由 iOS 系统沙盒机制保护。我们无法远程访问您 App 内的任何数据。

如果您开启了 iCloud 备份，iOS 系统可能会把 App 数据纳入 iCloud 本地备份，该行为由苹果系统控制，遵循 Apple 的隐私规则，不在我们的服务范围内。

四、未成年人保护

本应用适合 4 岁及以上用户使用，我们不会主动收集未成年人信息。

五、您的权利

您随时可以：
